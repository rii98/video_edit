# Technique cards

Distilled editing and motion-design practice, written to be **acted on**: rules with numbers (frames at 30 fps), the library components that implement them, and what to avoid.

How the cards were made:
- They come from web research (sources at the bottom of each card), cross-checked against long-standing film and broadcast conventions.
- Many sources are practitioner blogs, not standards. Treat the numbers as good starting points, then judge by eye.
- They record *principles* only, never copies of anyone's footage, brand assets or trademarks.

Add a card when you research a new technique. Keep the frontmatter: `ev library` lists `summary`.
