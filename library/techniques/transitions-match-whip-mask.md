# Transitions

## Rules
- **The default is a hard cut.** It's invisible and keeps pace. Add a transition only when it means something: a time jump, a reveal, or energy.
- **Match cut.** Line up a shape, position or motion across two shots (a circle becomes a circle, a hand swipe continues). Product showcases and cinematic edits use this to feel intentional.
- **Whip pan.**
  - Both shots blur in the **same direction at the same speed**, and the cut sits mid-blur.
  - Match the brightness and colour at the cut point.
  - High energy; for action, travel and social.
- **Mask / wipe reveals.** The new content is revealed *by* something: a shape, an object passing the lens, a growing circle. Use `MaskReveal` and keep it to 15–30 frames.
- **Timeline transitions** (between scenes in `timeline.json`):
  - `fade`: calm, time passing.
  - `slide`: sequential steps.
  - `wipe`: energetic.
  - Keep them to 12–20 frames.
- **Sound sells it.** A whoosh on a whip, a hit on a reveal. Audio continuity (J/L cuts) hides visual cuts.
- **Consistency.** Pick 1–2 transition styles per video and repeat them. Varied transitions look amateur.

## Avoid
- Star wipes, page curls and 3D cubes.
- Transitions on every cut.
- Transitions that are longer than the shots around them.
