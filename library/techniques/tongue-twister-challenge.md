# Tongue-twister challenge reels

## Rules
- **Hook (0–1.5 s):** state the challenge as numbers ("20 S-WORDS · 11 SEC") over the first frames. Numbers make it a test the viewer wants to try.
- **Word slam, one word at a time, exactly on the spoken onset.** Unlike keyword stickers (200–400 ms late), twister words must be frame-synced; speed is the joke. Pop 1.7 → 0.94 → 1 over 6 frames, alternate ±2–6° tilt. Paint the twisting sound (the S) in the accent colour.
- **Count the hard sounds** on a HUD (07/20). Bump scale 1.25 on each increment; a soft click per tick at 0.15–0.2 volume.
- **Cumulative cast:** every new noun becomes an on-screen object that stays (evidence board, sticker pile). Viral twister songs grow by adding characters; the screen should fill as the sentence does.
- **Punch-ins:** at most two, on the longest/hardest words (1.3–1.36×, arrive in 5 frames, hold ~20, release over ~14), with a whoosh landing on arrival.
- **Cut dead air and breaths** but keep the payoff gesture (glasses flip, point at camera).
- **Payoff:** score stamp ("20/20 ✓") over the breath jump cut, then "YOUR TURN 👉 / duet this" on a freeze of the point-at-camera. Challenge calls drive duets and comments.
- **Loop:** end with a 0.4–0.5 s rewind glitch (scanlines, shake, desaturate) so the restart feels intended.
- **Music:** a mischievous bed (pizzicato/sneaky, 85–120 BPM) ~15 dB under the voice; swell after the last word. CC-BY tracks need a credit line in the post caption.
