# Family-moment reels

## When to use
Unscripted footage of family or friends (a lesson, a prank, a chat) where the charm is the people. The edit should amplify their personalities, never bury them under graphics.

## Rules
- **Landscape source in 9:16: Fit, don't Fill.**
  - Filling 1080×1920 from a 16:9 clip keeps only ~32% of the width. With two people side by side, that cuts one of them out.
  - Fit the full frame at 1080 px wide (≈608 px tall). This also keeps a 1024 px phone clip at ~1:1, so it stays sharp.
  - Make the empty bands deliberate:
    - Either a designed surface (paper, colour, doodles),
    - or a blurred, scaled copy of the clip (40–80 px Gaussian, 70–85% opacity).
  - Black letterbox bars read as lazy.
- **Band jobs:**
  - **Top band (y 150–620):** the hook or title and the "chapter" label. Keep the top ~150 px clear for the TikTok username.
  - **Bottom band (y 1230–1570):** captions and reaction stickers. Keep the bottom ~350 px clear for the platform UI.
- **Punch-in on the punchline.**
  - Zoom 1.15–1.35× onto the face that delivers it.
  - Arrive in 4–6 frames (ease-out), hold through the laugh, ease back over 10–15 frames.
  - The whoosh's sharp end lands on the arrival frame.
  - At most one every ~8 s; more becomes noise.
- **Cut to the reaction.** The laugh or the kiss after a line is as important as the line. Hold on it ~1 s before moving on.
- **Hook in the first 1–2 s.** Show the premise as text ("Teaching my mom English") over the first frames. No logo intro.
- **Captions are the narrator** when there's no voiceover:
  - 2–4 words per block, with the active word highlighted.
  - Keep the speaker's actual words and accent. Mispronunciations are the joke: show them as said, then add the correction as a sticker, never a fix in the caption.
  - Emphasis words pop 200–400 ms after they're spoken.
- **Stickers and emoji:**
  - Pop in with overshoot: scale 0 → 1.15 → 1 over ~10 frames, with a 6–12° tilt.
  - Hold 1–2 s, then out in 6 frames.
  - Tie each one to a beat (heart on a hug, star on "star").
  - Max 2 on screen at once.
- **Real references:** when someone names a thing (a book, a place), show the real thing for 1.5–3 s as a "taped" or polaroid card with a 2–5° tilt.
- **Time jumps:** a full-screen card for 0.6–1.0 s ("A few moments later…" style). Make it original (your own type and colour): ripped meme clips get muted or claimed.
- **Trim dead air** over ~0.7 s between lines, but keep laughs and looks. Those are the content.
- **Music:** a light, bright bed (ukulele, whistle, pizzicato, 90–120 BPM), sitting 12–18 dB under the dialogue. Duck harder under speech; let it swell in the gaps and the outro.
- **SFX:** pop for stickers, whoosh for punch-ins and card moves, a soft "ding" for correct answers or reveals. Keep volume 0.3–0.6: it's a living-room video, not a trailer.
- **Outro (2–3 s):** a warm button (a freeze-frame of a hug or laugh, with a title) plus a follow line. Credit CC-BY music here or in the post caption.

## Avoid
- Graphics over faces.
- Correcting the person's English in a mocking tone. Celebrate the attempt.
- Every emoji at once.
- Copyrighted meme clips and songs.
